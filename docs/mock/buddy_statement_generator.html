Why buddy statements matter

Buddy statements are written by people who know you personally: family, friends, or the folks you served with. They give the VA a picture your medical records cannot: what you were like before, what happened, and how your condition affects the people close to you.

Family sees the daily impact on your life at home.
Service buddies saw what happened, or lived through the same events.
Friends and former officers can speak to who you were before service.

We recommend at least 3 statements, from at least 2 different perspectives.
